# Data sources and attribution

Raw datasets are not included in this repository. The public release publishes
derived metrics, cryptographic hashes, schemas, and accepted summaries needed
to audit the reported analysis.

## Diabetes Health Indicators

The Diabetes Health Indicators dataset was prepared by
[Alex Teboul](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/alexteboul/diabetes-health-indicators-dataset)
from the [CDC 2015 Behavioral Risk Factor Surveillance System
(BRFSS)](https://www.cdc.gov/brfss/annual_data/annual_2015.html). Credit is due
to both Alex Teboul for the prepared dataset and CDC BRFSS for the underlying
survey data.

- File used: `diabetes_binary_health_indicators_BRFSS2015.csv`
- Kaggle-listed license: CC0 1.0 / Public Domain

  `19f367e3e3350768f0c144c5d73ee5b355f67a57eaaa86ca7bd8aec594d8b1d0`
